# Security and privacy reports

Do not put sensitive details in a public issue. Until a private reporting
channel is selected, stop distribution or deployment, preserve only minimal
non-sensitive diagnostics, and contact the project owner through an already
established private channel.

For exposed credentials, revoke and rotate them immediately. For exposed
speaker data or identity, disable access, do not make additional copies, and
follow the incident procedure in `docs/privacy_and_consent.md`.
